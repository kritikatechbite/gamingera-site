About cookies

Cookies are small pieces of data stored by websites or related services. They can support sessions, settings and site performance.

What this demo uses

This starter site does not set tracking cookies through its own JavaScript and does not currently include advertising or analytics scripts. Your hosting platform and any services you later install may process technical data or set cookies.

Optional services

If you add analytics, advertising, chat, embedded videos or anti-bot verification, check their cookie and data practices. Obtain consent where required before placing non-essential cookies.

Managing cookies

You can remove or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may affect services that rely on them.
